Vilém Flusser and Louis Bec’s **paranatural treatise** uses the vampire squid (**Vampyroteuthis infernalis**) as a philosophical mirror to **critique human nature** and civilization. ### 1. Architectural Introduction: Bridging Physiology and Philosophy

Welcome to biological existentialism—an interdisciplinary field demonstrating how an organism's physical morphology directly determines its philosophical worldview (_Dasein_), epistemological logic, and cultural expressions. In _Vampyroteuthis Infernalis_, Vilém Flusser and Louis Bec argue that abstract thought is not a disembodied human privilege, but an organic extension of physical body plans, nervous architectures, and sensory apparatuses. The primary objective of this concept glossary is to demystify complex interdisciplinary terminology by connecting concrete biological structures to abstract existential modes of being. By contrasting human vertebrate anatomy with the radical, soft body plan of the abyss-dwelling vampire squid, this guide provides an information-architectural blueprint for comparing two divergent branches of life. Through this comparative mirror, we deconstruct anthropocentric assumptions regarding reason, art, memory, and spatial consciousness, revealing how physiological constraints dictate philosophical horizons. We begin our inquiry at the foundational taxonomic junctures where the evolutionary trajectories of human and vampyroteuthic existence originally branched.

### 3. Existential and Psychological Frameworks

#### **Dasein ("Being-in-the-World")**

- **Definition**: _Dasein_ denotes the concrete, dynamic relationship between the organism (_ego_) and its surrounding environment (_nonego_). Reality exists neither in the isolated subject nor in the external object, but in the reciprocal concurrence of both poles.
- **Human vs. Vampyroteuthic Dasein**:
  - _Human Dasein_: Conditioned by an upright posture on a solid terrestrial surface. Humans interact with the world by standing back, scanning open horizons with their eyes, and using freed hands to handle, manipulate, and dissect discrete objects. In Heideggerian terms, human _Dasein_ divides reality into things _present-at-hand_ (_Vorhandenheit_, raw nature) and _ready-to-hand_ (_Zuhandenheit_, tools/culture). Humans instinctively adopt a Platonic and Cartesian posture, operating under the assumption that hidden realities lie buried behind deceptive appearances (_aletheia_).
  - _Vampyroteuthic Dasein_: Suspended within a three-dimensional fluid volume of deep ocean darkness. Lacking a terrestrial floor, the vampire squid draws its environment centripetally toward its mouth and tentacles, experiencing space not as a flat surface to traverse, but as a dynamic, coiled spring of hydraulic pressure and impressionistic flow. It operates as a born Kantian: because its abyssal world is pitch black, its own bioluminescent emissions directly engender the phenomena it perceives.

#### **Character Armor (Reichian Model)**

- **Definition**: Adapted from Wilhelm Reich’s psychoanalytic model, character armor consists of the surface layers enveloping an organism that accumulate external environmental pressures and genotypic suppressions over its lifespan.
- **Biological-Psychological Link**: Accumulated suppressions are somatically sublimated into the animal's musculature, body posture, and chronic "muscle cramping," which outwardly manifests as individual "personality." The bioenergetic force stored within this armor is Reichian **orgone**, which explodes outward during orgasm or violent release.
- **Comparative Application**:

"An organism, then, is a phenotypic manifestation of these genotypic suppressions. It is a bomb, laden with potential energy, in which the sum of pressures, accumulated over the course of one life and over the course of the entire development of life, has been stored."

- _Human Armor_: The rigid vertebrate endoskeleton stabilizes character armor into a static, militant posture ("chest out!") driven by _thanatos_ (the death drive or stance of rigor mortis).
- _Vampyroteuthic Armor_: Lacking a rigid skeleton, _Vampyroteuthis_ exhibits a radical **buccal-anal conflation**—the anatomical fusion and extreme physical proximity of its mouth and anus. This anatomical conflation generates a volatile bioenergetic dynamic that oscillates wildly between militant rigidity (_thanatos_) and soft, orgastic absorption (_eros_, "belly out!"). It exists in a state of terminal, sadomasochistic tension, making love in order to make war, and driven by its cerebralized mouth to devour its own tentacles and anus in acts of suicidal cannibalism.

#### **Geist (Spirit / Mind / Mental Complexity)**

- **Definition**: _Geist_ is not a divine soul unique to humanity, but an emergent property of biological information processing and organic complexity present throughout life's overarching evolutionary agenda.
- **Asymmetric Spiritual Realization**:

"The natural state of human Dasein, on the contrary, is defined by universal hatred, by the universal struggle for survival... By overcoming its animality, therefore, the vampyroteuthis learns to hate; by overcoming ours, we learn to love. This overcoming can be called 'spirit' ('Geist'), and it expresses itself in the vampyroteuthis as hatred and in us as love."

- While human _Geist_ attempts to transcend its aggressive animality through love (_agape_), social collaboration, and utopian cultural structures, vampyroteuthic _Geist_ realizes itself through an anti-biological struggle against its own hereditary constraints. For _Vampyroteuthis_, spiritual elevation manifests as **hatred, suicidal anarchy, cannibalism, and "sins against the spirit" (**_**Sünde wider den Geist**_**)**—a total rejection of the biological rules governing its oviparous twin-clusters.

Because these internal psychological tensions (_thanatos_ and _eros_) cannot remain pent up within a soft, unarmored mantle, the organism requires specialized somatic organs and glandular secretions to translate its volatile _Dasein_ into outward epistemological expression.

### Analogy vs. Homology

- **[1] Physiological Mechanism**: _Analogy_ denotes organs with separate evolutionary origins that converge on identical functions due to environmental constraints. _Homology_ denotes organs sharing a common phylogenetic origin, regardless of how their anatomical functions have diverged.
- **[2] Philosophical Function**: Establishes a rigorous comparative framework for determining whether shared cognitive or behavioral capacities represent shared ancestral blueprints or convergent solutions to existential problems.
- **[3] Human Equivalent / Contrast**:
  - _Anatomical Structures_: The human photographic eye is _analogous_ to the cephalopod eye; human hands are _analogous_ to squid tentacles. Human perspiration (sweat) is _homologous_ to vampyroteuthic bioluminescence.
  - _Cephalopod Brain Stratification_: The cephalopod brain exhibits a complex dual stratification: its **lowermost brain levels are homologous** to ours (sharing a common phylogenetic origin with primitive ancestral worms, though thinking differently), whereas its **uppermost brain levels are analogous** to ours (originating separately through super-cerebralization, yet converging on complex intellectual capacity).

A systematic consolidation of these physiological, epistemological, and existential frameworks reveals the profound symmetry—and radical divergence—that defines the human and vampyroteuthic conditions.

## **Comparative Synthesis: Human Stance on Land vs. Vampyroteuthic Life in the Deep Sea Abyss** {#comparative-synthesis}

### 1. Introduction: The Mirror in the Deep

In his seminal treatise of biological existentialism, philosopher Vilém Flusser presents the abyssal vampire squid (_Vampyroteuthis infernalis_) not as a mere curiosity of marine biology, but as a radical counter-model—an _antimodel_—designed to dismantle human anthropocentric complacency. Operating under the classical Roman motto _De te fabula narratur_ ("of you the fable is told"), Flusser constructs a phenomenological fable that transforms the abyssal mollusk into a distorting mirror. By contemplating an organism that adopted an existential trajectory diametrically opposed to our own, we are forced to confront the contingency, absurdity, and repressed potentiality of human existence (_Dasein_).

Far from being utterly alien, the _Vampyroteuthis_ shares a foundational phylogenetic heritage with _Homo sapiens_. Both species belong to the subkingdom Metazoa ("true" multi-tissue animals) and the clade Bilateria, sharing a longitudinal axis that divides the body into symmetrical left and right halves. Crucially, both are Eucoelomata, possessing a true coelomic body cavity derived from three distinct embryonic germ layers: ectoderm, mesoderm, and endoderm. Hundreds of millions of years ago, the common ancestors of chordates and mollusks inhabited the primeval beach. From this shared shore, life diverged along two antipodal vectors: while our ancestral lineage ventured onto the firm terrestrial surfaces of the continents, the ancestral lineage of the _Vampyroteuthis_ descended into the crushing, lightless depths of the oceanic abyss.

**Key Philosophical Terminology**

- _**Dasein**_ **(Being-in-the-world):** The concrete, situated relationship between an organism (_ego_) and its surrounding environment (_nonego_), wherein neither pole can exist independently of their dynamic interaction.
- _**Mit-Sein**_ **(Co-being):** The condition of shared, intersubjective existence in which two distinct organisms occupy the same biological world, serving as dialectical mirrors that reveal each other's suppressed possibilities.

To make sense of our visceral revulsion toward this creature, Flusser proposes a core tenet of biological existentialism: _"Disgust recapitulates phylogenesis."_ The degree of existential disgust humans experience toward another organism directly corresponds to its distance from us on the phylogenetic tree. As soft-bodied mollusks that abandoned the segmented structure of annelids to slump into "squashy sacks," octopods occupy an apex of biological alienation for vertebrate humans. Yet, by resisting this instinctual revulsion, advanced learners can unmask the unexamined assumptions that govern human logic, culture, and ethics.

To understand how these divergent existential choices manifested, we must first analyze how their early evolutionary forks reshaped their physical and anatomical blueprints.

### 2. Anatomical Blueprint: The Land-Walker vs. The Abyssal Mollusk

The fundamental divergence between human and vampyroteuthic existence is rooted in an ancient embryological choice available to the Eucoelomata: the choice to refine either the **endoderm** (the digestive apparatus) or the **ectoderm** (the nervous and sensory system). Humans chose the ignoble path of the endoderm, constructing a history driven by the stomach, tool manipulation, and the energetic exploitation of the material world. The _Vampyroteuthis_ chose the path of the ectoderm, prioritizing the central nervous system and transforming its entire anatomy into a hyper-sensory, receptive apparatus.

This germ-layer divergence dictated their physical carriage and anatomical organization:

- **Posture & Orientation:** Humans erected their bodies vertically, executing a ninety-degree shift along their symmetrical axis. This stance elevated the head, freed the forelimbs from locomotion to manipulate tools, and lifted the eyes to scan distant horizons. Conversely, the _Vampyroteuthis_ abandoned the protective mollusk shell and adopted a "belly up, head down" orientation. It elevated its visceral mass while directing its head and mouth downward, merging its head and foot into a soft, centripetal organism that absorbs the world directly into its core.
- **Encirclement of the Brain:** The cephalopod brain is a complex, dual-hemispheric sphere divided into superesophageal and supraesophageal halves that directly encircle the esophagus. This layout carries a profound epistemological consequence: for the vampire squid, every swallowed portion of the world literally passes through the center of its brain. Cognition and digestion are structurally conflated; to know the world is to swallow and absorb it.
- **Eroticized Sensorium:** The male _Vampyroteuthis_ possesses three distinct penises, each adapted for specific functions during copulation—including a spoon-shaped organ that operates between the female's tongue-teeth and a thumb-shaped organ that caresses her abdomen. Furthermore, its sexual organs, photophores, and eyes are located directly upon its tentacles. Unlike humans, whose sensory processing must bridge separated hands, eyes, and reproductive organs, the _Vampyroteuthis_ receives unified optic, tactile, and erotic stimuli. Its mental state is an unbroken stream of Aristotelian shock, resolved exclusively through coitus and climax.

### 3. Environmental Realm: The Terrestrial Surface vs. The Abyssal Abyss

An organism's environmental realm (_Umwelt_) establishes the boundary conditions of its reality. Humans inhabit the firm, dry surface of the earth's continents—a two-dimensional, gaseous interface bathed in external sunlight and governed by a linear horizon. The _Vampyroteuthis_ inhabits the benthic ocean depths, an environment defined by liquid multidimensionality, crushing hydrostatic pressures, eternal night, and intense acoustic density.

The environmental dimensions that distinguish these two realms include:

- **Medium & Stance:** Humans stand upright on a solid, stationary continental floor, stepping across a 2D plane. The _Vampyroteuthis_ floats through a dense, fluid 3D volume. It propels itself not across surfaces, but through volumes, utilizing its siphon (_hyponome_) like a water-jet to bore through watery space like a dynamic screw. Lacking a firm ground beneath it, it cannot conceive of static, stationary objects ("problems") to be stepped over or handled.
- **Illumination & Vision:** Humans practice a passive vision, perceiving an already-illuminated world lit by the distant sun. The _Vampyroteuthis_ resides beneath the photic zone, immersed in an eternal night where light exists only when generated by living beings. It practices an active vision, projecting self-generated cones of bioluminescence (_photophores_) into the dark to temporarily illuminate and synthesize its reality.
- **Acoustics & Sensation:** Gaseous air provides relatively low acoustic resistance to human ears. In contrast, the high hydrostatic pressures (up to 1,000 atmospheres) and salt concentrations of the deep ocean transform seawater into a dense, hyper-conductive medium. The abyss reverberates with incessant, deafening sound waves, wrapping the creature in a fluid sensory soup of pressure, chemical gradients, and acoustic vibrations.

These environmental constraints do not merely dictate physical survival; they directly condition the organism's modes of perception, cognitive processing, and cultural creation.

### 4. Perception, Thought, and Culture: Dissecting Reason vs. Bioluminescent Seduction

The cognitive and cultural architectures of humans and vampire squids present inverse methods of encountering reality. While human thought is rooted in analytical distance and the physical manipulation of inert matter, vampyroteuthic culture operates through sensory absorption, glandular emissions, and orgasmic intersubjectivity.

The primary distinctions between these two civilizational modes encompass four key domains:

- **Platonic/Cartesian Dissection vs. Kantian/Impressionistic Seduction:** Human cognition relies on the "scalpel of reason." Inheriting the hand of the tree-dwelling primate, humans dissect appearances into discrete concepts, establishing an analytical boundary between subject and object. Humans are born Platonists, assuming a hidden truth resides behind deceiving appearances. The _Vampyroteuthis_, lacking hands to hold or dissect objects, operates via "light-reason." It projects illuminated cones into the dark, grasping incoming impressions directly with its tentacles. It does not dissect reality; its mind performs an immediate, preconceptual critique of impressions, treating the world not as static truth, but as a seductive, impressionistic display.
- **The Taxonomy of Glandular Epistemology:** Lacking external tools or spoken words, the _Vampyroteuthis_ communicates and stores culture intersubjectively through a highly articulated system of four distinct body glands:
  1. _Chromatophores:_ Controlled directly by the brain, these skin pigment cells emit rapid, intraspecific color displays that articulate internal emotional and intellectual states.

By broadcasting synthetic images that bypass logic to directly capture the receiver's brain, modern digital media is abandoning Cartesian dissection and becoming **increasingly vampyroteuthic**.

The natural state of the _Vampyroteuthis_ is the exact inverse: its baseline existence is one of continuous erotic union, passion, and mutual absorption (_Eros_). ## **The Abyss as Mirror: A Phenomenological and Existential Critique of _Vampyroteuthis infernalis_ as Human Anti-Model** {#abyss-mirror}

### 1. Philosophical Foundations: The Fable as Non-Anthropocentric Phenomenology

#### 1.1. Introduction & Methodological Context

To construct a rigorous phenomenological critique of human existence (_Dasein_), philosophy must locate a site of radical alterity—a co-being (_Mit-Sein_) that shares our fundamental biological origins while embodying an inverted, anti-human project of consciousness. The abyssal cephalopod _Vampyroteuthis infernalis_ provides precisely such an existential counter-model. To engage this creature, however, we must subvert traditional scientific objectivity, which pretends to observe the living world from an impossible position of pure transcendence. Far from achieving neutral detachment, scientific rationality is itself a mammalian function—an endodermal extension analogous to digestion. Just as the mammal ingests, breaks down, and assimilates external matter within its digestive tract, scientific objectivity seeks to crush, categorize, and digest phenomena into quantifiable data points. The further removed a phenomenon is from human self-recognition, the more "objectively" it can be described; yet this very distance renders the object repulsive, terrifying, or completely uninteresting. Pure scientific detachment thus degenerates into an unreflective mammalian mania that isolates humanity within its own anthropocentric mirror.

To bypass this epistemological impasse, we adopt the methodology of the philosophical "fable," explicitly drawing upon and subverting the empirical scaffolds of zoological taxonomy—such as the paranaturalist records of Louis Bec and the _Institut Scientifique de Recherche Paranaturaliste_ (ISRP), whose monographs on _Vampyroteuthis infernalis g._, _Lumanter phusagrion_, and _Akroate hadal f._ parodicly interrogate the boundaries of empirical zoology. Phenomenology allows us to approach _Vampyroteuthis_ not as a dead object floating in formalin, but as an intentional subject co-existing within the shared biosphere. Guided by the classical dictum _De te fabula narratur_ ("of you the tale is told"), evaluating human _Dasein_ from the perspective of a deep-sea mollusk provides a radically distanced yet thoroughly non-transcendent critique. It does not attempt to vault out of the world into a divine ether; rather, it gazes across at human Dasein from the dark, pressurized depths of an aquatic mirror.

**Methodological Directive:** Scientific detachment is a mammalian endodermal bias that digests reality into abstract quantities. It must be replaced by a non-transcendent phenomenological fable (_De te fabula narratur_). By treating the biological organism as a stratified memory of existence, this fable deploys the radical alterity of _Vampyroteuthis infernalis_ to dismantle the anthropocentric biases, somatic character armors, and historical fictions of human _Dasein_.
