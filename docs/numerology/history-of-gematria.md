### 1. Introduction to Alphanumeric Systems: Substitution vs. Gematria

Alphanumeric systems bridge language and mathematics by assigning numerical values to individual letters of an alphabet. Historically, cryptographers, scholars, and esoteric philosophers devised these frameworks to serve distinct purposes, ranging from secret military intelligence to symbolic literary encoding. To analyze or construct these systems accurately, a learner must first grasp the foundational operational distinction between a **substitution cipher** and a **gematria calculation**.

- **Substitution Cipher:** A cryptographic method of character replacement where each letter in a word is converted into a distinct numerical code or symbol while preserving the original spatial sequence of the text. The string length remains identical to the input word, producing a continuous series of numbers rather than a single sum.
- **Gematria System:** An analytical or interpretive system where the individual numerical values of a word's letters are added together to compress the textual unit into a single mathematical sum.

| Mechanism / Method      | Primary Function & Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
